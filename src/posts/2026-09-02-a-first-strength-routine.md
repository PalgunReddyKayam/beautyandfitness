Strength training is the single most useful form of exercise for long-term health, and it needs far less time than most beginners assume. Two sessions a week is enough to build meaningful strength and muscle in the first year. This routine covers the whole body in each session so nothing is missed if life gets in the way.

## The routine

Do this twice a week with at least one rest day between sessions. Both days are the same.

| Movement | Sets × reps | What it trains |
|---|---|---|
| Goblet squat | 3 × 8–10 | Legs, core |
| Push-up (or incline push-up) | 3 × 8–12 | Chest, shoulders, arms |
| Dumbbell Romanian deadlift | 3 × 8–10 | Hamstrings, glutes, back |
| One-arm dumbbell row | 3 × 10 each side | Upper back, biceps |
| Dumbbell overhead press | 3 × 8–10 | Shoulders |
| Plank | 3 × 30–45 seconds | Core |

Rest 90 seconds to two minutes between sets. Warm up with five minutes of easy movement and one lighter set of the first exercise.

## The one rule for progress

When you can complete all sets at the top of the rep range with good form, add weight next session — the smallest increase available. If you can't hit the bottom of the range, the weight is too heavy; drop it. That's the whole system, and it works for years.

## What good form means

Slow enough to control the weight the whole way. A full range of motion — thighs at least parallel in the squat, chest to an inch from the floor in the push-up. If you can't do that yet, use less weight or an easier variation. Form beats load every time.

## Why not five days a week

Muscle is built between sessions, not during them. Beginners recover more slowly and adapt faster than experienced lifters, so more frequency mostly adds fatigue. Add a third day after three or four months if you want to, not before.

## What to do this week

- Book two 45-minute slots in your calendar and treat them like appointments.
- Do the routine with weights that feel comfortably light. The first two weeks are for learning the movements.
- Write down what you lifted. Progress is only visible if you track it.
