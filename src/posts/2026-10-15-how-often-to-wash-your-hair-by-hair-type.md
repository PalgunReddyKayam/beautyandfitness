Most people settle on a washing routine around the age of fifteen and never revisit it. They wash daily because that is what the household did, or once a week because someone told them daily washing was damaging. Neither habit was chosen with their own hair in mind.

There is no correct number. There is a sensible range for each hair type, and a few signs that you are outside it. Greasy roots by mid-afternoon suggest the gaps are too long. A tight, itchy scalp, or lengths that feel like straw, suggest you may be washing more often than your hair needs, or using a shampoo that is too harsh for it.

The advice below comes from dermatology guidance and stylist practice. Nobody has run good trials on the ideal washing frequency, so treat the ranges as starting points.

## Hair type sets the range

- **Fine, straight hair:** every one to two days. Oil slides down a smooth strand quickly, and fine hair shows it fast.
- **Wavy or thick hair:** two to four times a week.
- **Curly hair:** once or twice a week, sometimes three times.
